# Prompt writing for AI text-to-3D / image-to-3D generators feeding Blender (state as of 2026-09-27)

Scope note: research date 2026-09-27. "Observed" means I read the live page or public JSON on that date. Vendor blogs (Tripo, Meshy, Hyper3D) are marketing material and are flagged where they are the only source. The reader is a solo developer making flat-shaded, no-outline, pastel "toy" low-poly characters (about 2.5 heads tall, dot eyes, blush) and props in Blender 5.2, rendered to sprites for Unity.

## 1. Official prompt guidance of the main generators (structure, keywords, negatives, presets, polycount/topology, pose, symmetry)

### Takeaway
Every vendor now recommends the same shape of prompt: one subject first, 2–6 concrete shape/material/colour details, style words last, no quality-spam and (mostly) no negative prompts. The real control for game use has moved out of the prompt into settings: pose mode (A/T), target face count, quad vs triangle, "smart/low-poly topology" modes, texture on/off, PBR/de-lighting. As of Sept 2026 the dedicated low-poly modes are Meshy "Smart Topology" (Meshy-6+), Tripo "Smart Mesh" P1.0 → P2.0 (native quads, launched 2026-09-21), Rodin Gen-2.5 "Smart Low-Poly"/Quad, and Tencent Hunyuan3D "PolyGen".

### Cited Findings

**Meshy (Meshy-6 geometry Jan 2026; Meshy 7 / "meshy-7.1" is current)**
- Official formula: "[Subject] + [Material/Texture] + [Art Style] + [Technical Constraints]"; worked example split into "A medieval knight's helmet" / "with scratched iron surface" / "in dark fantasy art style" / "low-poly, game-ready asset". Early words carry the most weight. Style keywords the guide names: Pixar style, Studio Ghibli, flat shading cel-shaded, low-poly, game-ready, stylized/cartoon, chibi, smart-topology. Official "good" example verbatim: "A sci-fi plasma rifle with glowing blue energy core, carbon fiber body with chrome accents, cyberpunk style, game-ready low-poly asset" (no date on page) — [Meshy Docs: 3D Prompting Guide](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/guides/prompting)
- Help-center guide: "Aim for 20–60 words"; subject first, then critical modifiers, style keywords at the end; negative prompts are "not supported; use positive descriptors instead"; avoid conflicting style keywords; to avoid floating parts write "solid construction, single solid mesh, or unified form"; poses: T-pose, A-pose; game-ready vocabulary "low poly, game-ready, optimized topology, PBR textures"; styles listed include "Low poly with flat shading" and "Cell shaded/anime style" (page "updated over a month ago" as of 2026-09-27) — [Meshy Help: Prompt Guide](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/11972484-meshy-prompt-guide-best-practices-and-templates-by-asset-type)
- API (text-to-3D): `prompt` max 800 characters; `model_type` = standard / smart-topology / lowpoly (lowpoly deprecated); `ai_model` = meshy-6-lite, meshy-6, meshy-7.1, latest; `topology` quad or triangle (default); `target_polycount` 100–300,000 with remesh, or 100–15,000 with smart-topology; `pose_mode` a-pose / t-pose / ""; `remove_lighting` boolean in refine (Meshy-6 only); `texture_prompt` and `texture_image_url` for texturing; deprecated: `negative_prompt`, `art_style` (realistic/sculpture, unsupported by Meshy-6), `symmetry_mode`, `is_a_t_pose`, `hd_texture` — [Meshy API: Text to 3D](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/text-to-3d)
- Remove-lighting "removes highlights and shadows from the base color texture"; supported for meshy-6/meshy-7.1/latest in text-to-3D, meshy-6 only for image-to-3D API (per search summary of docs) — [Meshy API: Image to 3D](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/image-to-3d)
- Low-poly tutorial (dated 2026-07-10): Low Poly Mode shipped with Meshy-6 in January 2026 and is now labelled "Smart Topology"; low-poly prompt words "low poly", "simple shapes", "flat shading", "geometric", "vibrant colors", "retro video game style"; example "A small wizard tower with a spiral staircase, moss on the stones, glowing blue windows. Low poly style, flat shading, vibrant colors."; polycount table: under 5K = mobile AR/casual, 5K–20K = mobile games, 20K–50K = PC/console; quad "for further editing and rigging in Blender", triangle "for direct use in engines"; key caveat "Smart Topology solves the polygon budget, not the look. The mesh renders smooth by default." — [Meshy Tutorial: Make Low Poly 3D Models](https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/make-low-poly-3d-models)
- Meshy added a user-selectable A-pose/T-pose to image-to-3D in December 2025 — [AlternativeTo news, 2025-12](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/12/meshy-lets-users-pick-a-pose-or-t-pose-in-image-to-3d-workflow)

**Tripo (VAST) — H3.0/H3.1 high-poly models; Smart Mesh P1.0 (Mar 2026) and P2.0 (Sept 2026) low-poly models**
- API text_to_model: `prompt` max 1024 characters "equivalent to approximately 100 words", no emojis; `negative_prompt` supported, max 255 characters; `model_version` `v3.1-20260211` or `v3.0-20250812`; `texture` (set false to disable texturing), `pbr` default true, `texture_quality` standard/detailed, `geometry_quality` standard/detailed, `quad` (+5 credits), `face_limit`, `smart_low_poly` "hand-crafted topology" (+10 credits), `generate_parts` segmented editable model (+20 credits), `export_uv` — [Tripo OpenAPI docs: Text to model (H3)](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/model-generation/text-to-model-v3-0-v3-1.html)
- `face_limit` ranges: with smart_low_poly=true 1,000–20,000; with quad also true 500–10,000; quad=true without face_limit defaults to 10,000; smart_low_poly "inputs with less complexity work best" — [Tripo platform docs (via search)](https://platform.tripo3d.ai/docs/generation)
- Legacy `style` enum in the Python SDK (applicability to v3.x not documented in H3 docs): `"person:person2cartoon"`, `"animal:venom"`, `"object:clay"`, `"object:steampunk"`, `"object:christmas"`, `"object:barbie"`, `"gold"`, `"ancient_bronze"` — [tripo-python-sdk API.md](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/tripo-python-sdk/blob/master/docs/API.md)
- Smart Mesh P1.0 (blog 2026-03-11): "clean, structured low-poly topology directly during generation", ~2 s generation; H3.1 = hero/high-poly, Smart Mesh = real-time assets — [Tripo blog: Introducing Smart Mesh P1.0](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/introducing-smart-mesh-v1)
- P2.0 (preview blog 2026-08-19; official launch press release 2026-09-21): native quad topology; auto-separated parts ("body, clothing, and accessories into independent pieces"); triangles 500–50,000 faces, quads 500–25,000; four-view input (front/left/right/back); up to 4 variants at different face counts per generation; Mesh Edit regional regeneration — [Tripo blog: P2.0](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/tripo-p2-0-preview); [PR Newswire via Morningstar, 2026-09-21](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260921sf53561/tripo-ai-releases-latest-model-tripo-p20-advancing-ai-3d-generation-with-production-ready-assets)
- Tripo blog prompt anatomy (2026-03-10, vendor SEO article): four parts in order — core subject & form; style & fidelity (e.g. "stylized PBR game asset, 5k tris, clean quad-dominant topology"); 2–4 key details + view ("front view"/"three-quarter view"); exclusions/technical ("no cape, symmetrical, manifold, single mesh"). Humanoid example verbatim: "Town guard NPC, stocky male humanoid, low-poly stylized model for mobile game, wearing a kettle helmet and brigandine armor, holding a spear, neutral stance, front view, no facial features needed." Stylized switch words: "hand-painted aesthetic, bold shapes, exaggerated proportions, simplified details, albedo texture ready"; "separated into groups"/"logically separated" to get separable parts — [Tripo blog: prompt templates](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/ai-3d-model-generator-prompt-templates-for-specific-asset-types)
- Tripo low-poly blog (2026-03-10, vendor): be "technical and descriptive" — Shape "Wide, squat treasure chest with heavy metal bands.", Style "Low poly, faceted, stylized, no smooth shading.", Constraint "Under 2000 triangles, clean geometry."; pitfall: do not combine "highly detailed" with "low poly" — prompt "low poly barrel, implied wood planks with texture"; AI "struggles with precise symmetry" — [Tripo blog: generate low poly assets](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/how-to-generate-low-poly-assets-with-ai)

**Hyper3D Rodin (Deemos) — Gen-2 (2025), Gen-2.5 (launched 2026-05-26, "0702" update)**
- Gen-2.5 API: `tier` Gen-2.5-Extreme-Low … Gen-2.5-Extreme-High ("Always send a Gen-2.5 tier explicitly"); `prompt` for text-to-3D (omit when sending images); 1–5 images; `mesh_mode` (Raw documented), `texture_mode`, `addons: HighPack` (2K→4K), `geometry_instruct_mode: creative`; example prompt "A small wooden treasure chest with brass trim" — [Hyper3D Docs: Rodin Gen-2.5](https://docs.hyper3d.ai/en/api-specification/rodin-gen2-5)

- Gen-2 quad presets 4K/8K/18K/50K Quad (18K default for text-to-3D); T/A-pose rest-pose conditioning for humanoids; example "A stylized low-poly fantasy potion bottle with a glowing teal liquid and an ornate cork wrapped in twine." (third-party knowledge base) — [Scenario KB: Rodin essentials](https://help.scenario.com/articles/6155258011-rodin-hyper3d-models-the-essentials)
- 0702 update: better high-tier geometry, 8K textures for all users, emissive "Bloom" texture, free for everyone — [Hyper3D on X](https://x.com/DeemosTech/status/2074192385048039835)
- Official low-poly style page (observed 2026-09-27): "Words like 'low poly', 'flat-shaded', 'faceted', and 'one color per face' steer the geometry and the texture. Verbatim recipes: "Low-poly fox, flat-shaded triangular facets, warm autumn palette, strong silhouette, clean surface detail."; "low poly desert ruin archway, sandstone blocks, flat shading, three-tone palette, modular kit piece for a mobile game"; "low poly delivery drone, hard edges, faceted panels, two-tone gray and orange palette, readable at a distance, no decals"; "low poly alpine cottage, snow-capped roof, flat colors, compact square footprint, designed for an isometric camera" — [Hyper3D: Low Poly style](https://hyper3d.ai/styles/low-poly)
- Official cartoon style page (observed 2026-09-27): three traits — rounded silhouette, exaggerated proportions, bright matte colour. Verbatim: "cartoon treasure chest, chunky proportions, rounded edges, gold trim, game prop, white background"; "cartoon wizard cat, big head, small body, chunky staff, saturated purple palette, full body." (A-pose selected separately in settings); "cartoon watering can, cherry red body, soft yellow spout, matte plastic look, white background" = "the toy-plastic recipe"; "cartoon burger food truck, rounded body, oversized wheels, bright teal and yellow, giant ketchup bottle on the roof, white background"; advice "Keep the polycount mid-range … If you want facets on purpose, that is low poly"; "Ask for 'rounded edges, chunky proportions'" so softness is in the mesh — [Hyper3D: Cartoon style](https://hyper3d.ai/styles/cartoon)

**Tencent Hunyuan3D (open 2.0/2.1; hosted 3.x; PolyGen)**
- Official Global Prompt Guide (updated 2026-01-21): format "{quantifier, recommend 1}+{subject}, {selectable: detailed description}, {selectable: style}"; single primary subject; details = shape, colour, material, style; "no need to include frequently used prompt skills like negative prompt or 4k"; examples verbatim "A deep brown, big-eyed owl." and "A yellow dress printed with yellow and green patterns, presenting an authentic photography style." — [Tencent Cloud: HY 3D Global Prompt Guide](https://www.tencentcloud.com/document/product/1284/75290)
- Tencent Cloud techpedia: subject concretely + two or three silhouette details + surface described "the way you would to a texture artist"; "Vague prompts produce vague geometry; overloaded prompts produce confused geometry"; generate one object per prompt and assemble downstream (search summary) — [Tencent Cloud techpedia: Best prompts](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/144716)
- Hunyuan3D-PolyGen (announced July 2025): autoregressive "art-grade" retopology producing quad topology; Tencent Cloud's Smart Topology API now uses PolyGen 1.5 with triangle or mixed tri/quad output and high/medium/low reduction levels — [Tencent Hunyuan on X](https://x.com/TencentHunyuan/status/1942174221976981881); [Scenario: Hunyuan PolyGen 1.5](https://www.scenario.com/models/hunyuan-polygen-15)
- Hunyuan3D 2.0 paper describes a low-polygon stylization module (vertex merging + texture preservation) — [arXiv 2501.12202](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.12202)

**Microsoft TRELLIS.2 (Dec 2025)**: 4B-parameter, image-only input; GLB with PBR; export has a `decimation_target` (example 1,000,000); ≥24 GB VRAM; MIT licence — [GitHub microsoft/TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2). No text prompt, so no prompt guide applies.

### Inferences
- For the user's faceted pastel toy look, the vendors converge on a vocabulary: "low poly, flat-shaded, faceted, one color per face, simple shapes, N-tone palette, strong silhouette, chunky proportions, rounded edges, matte plastic, white background, full body, A-pose/T-pose". "Cartoon/toy" words push toward smooth mid-poly meshes (Rodin's own page says so), so "toy" and "low poly" should be combined deliberately and the face budget should come from a setting, not from words.
- Negative prompts are effectively dead on Meshy and discouraged on Hunyuan; Tripo still accepts a 255-character `negative_prompt`. Community practice (section 2) is to put exclusions inside the positive prompt ("no text, no base, no floating elements").
- Pose and polycount are now settings on all three commercial leaders; writing "T-pose" in the prompt matters mainly for image-generation of the concept (section 3).

### Gaps
- No official Meshy/Tripo/Rodin page defines a "chibi" or "2.5-heads-tall" proportion keyword; head-to-body ratio control is not documented anywhere I found.
- Rodin Gen-2.5 API full parameter list for `TAPose`, `quality_override`, `material` (PBR vs Shaded) could not be confirmed from the fetched Gen-2.5 page; only the press release and Gen-2 third-party docs mention them.
- Tripo's `style` presets are documented only in the older SDK; I could not confirm they work with v3.x or P2.0.
- Hunyuan3D 3.x hosted-service prompt settings (face count, "LowPoly/Geometry" generate types) were not documented in the fetched guide.

## 2. Most-liked / featured community results and their verbatim prompts

### Takeaway
Only Meshy exposes prompts at scale. Its public community feed (read on 2026-09-27) shows that the most-liked 2026 work is overwhelmingly image-to-3D (about 94% of 2026 posts in the sample) and that popular posts are dense showcase meshes (hundreds of thousands to about 2M triangles), not low-poly game assets. Tripo's featured gallery exposes no prompts (all 18 featured items had an empty prompt field). I did not find a public Rodin community gallery with prompts.

### Cited Findings

**Method and aggregate signals (Meshy, observed 2026-09-27)**
- Meshy's Discover page loads posts from a public JSON endpoint: `https://www.meshy.ai/meshyd-api/web/public/community/posts?sortBy=likes&pageNum=1&pageSize=50` (also `isFeatured=true&sortBy=popularity`, `sortBy=downloads`, `sortBy=trending`). Each post includes stats (views, downloads, remixes, likes) and `modelArgs` (prompt, image input, model codename, model type, topology) — [Meshy Discover](https://www.meshy.ai/discover). Individual post URL pattern: `https://www.meshy.ai/posts/<id>`.
- I pulled 2,000 records (1,773 unique posts): top 1,500 by likes, 300 featured-by-popularity, 200 by downloads. Of the 1,773, 1,566 (88%) were generated from an input image; 94 of the top 100 by likes were image-based; 93.7% of the 1,291 posts published in 2026 were image-based — [Meshy community endpoint](https://www.meshy.ai/meshyd-api/web/public/community/posts?sortBy=likes&pageNum=1&pageSize=50)
- Model mix in that sample: codename "avocado" 1,098 posts, meshy-5.3 279, "blueberry" 131, meshy-5 121, meshy-4 58. Post pages decode these: "avocado" = "Meshy 6 (geometry) + Meshy 7 (texture)" on the koi post, "blueberry" = "Meshy 7" on the Pip post — [koi post](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019feedb-3e5b-72eb-b522-2e59d4897e71); [Pip post](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019ff5f8-d491-73a3-8d1f-d7c42bd049dc)
- Only 5 of 1,773 popular posts used `modelType: lowpoly` (Smart Topology); nearly all popular models are "standard" dense meshes — same endpoint.
- The top-liked posts expose only a title, not a prompt: "The Yelling Goblin" (1,154 likes, 165 downloads, 5,232 views, featured, 2026-08-04, Meshy 6, image input); "Moonlit Wolf Pup" (1,118 likes, 159 downloads, 4,091 views, featured, 2026-08-02, image input) — [Yelling Goblin](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019fca8b-b1fe-7bdb-92c3-afed0c5a602a); [Moonlit Wolf Pup](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019fc450-cb57-7627-837c-9afc7ee2ff74)

For image posts the long descriptive "Name" text is what the API returns as `prompt` (it is probably the prompt used to make the reference image, but Meshy does not label it so). ## 3. Text-to-3D vs image-to-3D (concept image first) for stylized characters, and concept-image prompts

### Takeaway
Image-to-3D is the de facto route for high-quality stylized characters in 2026: vendors' own guides tell users to make a clean concept image (front view, full body, neutral/A/T pose, white background, even lighting), and about 94% of popular 2026 Meshy posts were image-based. Multi-view input (front/side/back) is the newest lever (Tripo P2.0 four-view input; Meshy multi-image; Rodin up to 5 images).

### Cited Findings
- Meshy official input-image guidance: plain white or transparent background; front-facing or slightly angled; for characters "T-pose or neutral standing pose produces more usable topology"; "Even, diffused lighting" and avoid directional light that bakes shadows; ≥512 px, ideally ≥1024 px; subject fills 70–90% of frame; for images made in Midjourney/DALL-E add "white background, studio lighting, front view, product photography style"; multi-view (front/side/back/¾) reduces hallucinated unseen sides — [Meshy Help: better image-to-3D results](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/15723519-how-to-get-better-image-to-3d-results-in-meshy)
- Tripo Nano Banana workflow (blog dated 2026-07-28): concept-image style suffixes verbatim — characters "full-body character render, centered, neutral pose"; products "front-facing product photo, isolated on white background, studio lighting"; architecture "isometric 3D render, plain white background"; general template "Front-facing, centered, isolated on white background, even lighting, no shadows."; avoid dramatic camera angles, motion blur, strong reflections, cinematic effects; Smart Mesh ≈5,000 faces by default vs HD Model up to ~2M; exporting v3.0/v3.1 models requires a paid subscription — [Tripo blog: Nano Banana image-to-3D](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/nano-banana-image-to-3d-workflow)
- Tripo P2.0 accepts front, left, right and back references and "uses all 4 angles to reconstruct geometry" — [Tripo blog: P2.0](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/tripo-p2-0-preview)
- Rodin Gen-2.5 accepts 1–5 images; the first image drives material generation — [Hyper3D Docs](https://docs.hyper3d.ai/en/api-specification/rodin-gen2-5); Rodin's own low-poly page says an image of an existing low-poly asset "locks the direction even harder" than words — [Hyper3D: Low Poly](https://hyper3d.ai/styles/low-poly)
- Third-party workflow write-ups: an unoccluded A-pose/T-pose prevents limbs merging with the torso, a high-contrast solid background helps edge detection, and multi-view sheets (front/side/back) made with Nano Banana feed Tripo (low-quality marketing blog; treat as anecdotal) — [selfielab blog, 2026-02-19](https://selfielabstudio.com/blog/nano-banana-to-3d-figures-game-character-workflow-20260219)
- Tripo "remove baked lighting" guide: start from reference images with flat neutral lighting; negative terms such as "directional light," "drop shadow," "specular highlight"; PBR maps separate albedo from lighting; Magic Brush repaints leftover shadows (vendor page, undated) — [Tripo: remove baked lighting](https://www.tripo3d.ai/game-development/remove-baked-lighting-shadows-image-to-3d-ai-game-assets)
- Community evidence: 1,566 of 1,773 popular Meshy posts (88%), 94 of the top 100 by likes, and 93.7% of 2026 posts used an input image (my count from the public feed, 2026-09-27) — [Meshy community endpoint](https://www.meshy.ai/meshyd-api/web/public/community/posts?sortBy=likes&pageNum=1&pageSize=50)
- Tripo gallery: featured items are image-based with "ai_enhancement" images (Tripo generates extra views/cleaned images) — [Tripo Studio Explore](https://studio.tripo3d.ai/explore)

### Inferences
- Suggested concept-image prompt for the user's character (assembled from the cited vendor wording, not tested): "[subject], cute toy figure, about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, tiny body, dot eyes, pink blush circles, pastel [colour] palette, matte plastic, low poly flat-shaded facets, full body, front view, A-pose, arms slightly away from body, centered, plain white background, even flat lighting, no shadows". Then a matching side and back view for multi-view input.
- Dot eyes and blush are texture-level details at this scale; generators will bake them into the albedo (or model them as bumps). For flat-colour sprites it is probably cleaner to generate geometry without texture (Tripo `texture=false`, Meshy preview/untextured, Hunyuan geometry-only) and add eyes/blush as separate small meshes or material slots in Blender.
- Keep the concept images consistent across a roster (same camera, same lighting, same palette) because every vendor stresses consistent references; that is the practical way to control style across many characters.

### Gaps
- No controlled study (only vendor claims and my Meshy count) comparing text-only vs image-first output quality for stylized low-poly characters.
- No official statement from any vendor on how well head-to-body ratio from a concept image survives generation for chibi proportions.

## 4. Vocabulary that works/fails for low-poly, pastel, toy, chibi, vinyl, clay; reducing polycount, removing baked lighting, converting to flat colours in Blender

### Takeaway
Words alone do not make a low-poly mesh: default generations are dense (hundreds of thousands to ~2M triangles) and smooth-shaded, and "highly detailed" fights "low poly". Use the generator's low-poly/smart-topology mode with a target face count, then in Blender: Shade Flat, decimate or remesh if needed, and bake the diffuse colour (Color only, no lighting) to an image or directly to a colour attribute, then flatten to a palette.

### Cited Findings
**What works (vendor vocabulary)**
- Low poly: "low poly", "flat-shaded", "faceted", "one color per face" — [Hyper3D: Low Poly](https://hyper3d.ai/styles/low-poly); "low poly", "simple shapes", "flat shading", "geometric", "vibrant colors", "retro video game style" — [Meshy tutorial](https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/make-low-poly-3d-models); "Low poly, faceted, stylized, no smooth shading." + "Under 2000 triangles, clean geometry." — [Tripo blog](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/how-to-generate-low-poly-assets-with-ai)
- Cartoon/toy: "chunky proportions, rounded edges", "big head, small body", "matte plastic look", saturated matte colour ("the toy-plastic recipe") — [Hyper3D: Cartoon](https://hyper3d.ai/styles/cartoon)
- Toy/vinyl/clay/plush wording from popular community posts: "Pop Mart style chibi … Glossy painted vinyl finish", "matte vinyl finish", "Matte clay surface", "vibrant clay style", "dreamy collectible toy aesthetic … smooth rounded geometry", "compact toy-like proportions" — see section 2 items 4–10 ([example](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019a0576-18e1-761a-ac1c-fbc2dfb2a3fe))

**What fails / caveats**
- "Do not ask for 'highly detailed' and 'low poly' in the same prompt"; fine details should be implied by texture, e.g. "low poly barrel, implied wood planks with texture"; AI "struggles with precise symmetry" — [Tripo blog](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/how-to-generate-low-poly-assets-with-ai)
- "Smart Topology solves the polygon budget, not the look. The mesh renders smooth by default." — [Meshy tutorial](https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/make-low-poly-3d-models)
- Rodin: cartoon curves "need enough polygons to stay round — visible faceting breaks the illusion"; faceted is a different style — [Hyper3D: Cartoon](https://hyper3d.ai/styles/cartoon)
- Default outputs observed on Meshy community posts: 1,946,728 triangles (Pip, Meshy 7 image-to-3D) and 387,476 triangles (koi, Meshy 6 text-to-3D) — [Pip](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019ff5f8-d491-73a3-8d1f-d7c42bd049dc); [koi](https://www.meshy.ai/posts/019feedb-3e5b-72eb-b522-2e59d4897e71)
- AI meshes typically arrive with too many triangles (example "500,000"), non-manifold pieces and UVs that break once the mesh is simplified (tutorial dated 2026-07-07) — [Triverse: clean up an AI model in Blender](https://triverse.ai/blog/clean-up-ai-model-in-blender-tutorial)

**Reducing polycount at the source**
- Meshy smart-topology target 100–15,000; Tripo smart_low_poly 1,000–20,000 (quad 500–10,000); Tripo P2.0 tris 500–50,000 / quads 500–25,000 with up to 4 face-count variants per run; Rodin Gen-2.5 face count from 500; Hunyuan PolyGen 1.5 high/medium/low reduction — [Meshy API](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/text-to-3d); [Tripo docs](https://platform.tripo3d.ai/docs/generation); [Tripo P2.0](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/tripo-p2-0-preview); [Rodin press release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/2134263/hyper3d-launches-rodin-gen-2-5-bringing-sculpt-level-detail-and-production-control-to-ai-3d-generation); [Scenario: PolyGen 1.5](https://www.scenario.com/models/hunyuan-polygen-15)
- Tripo P2.0 auto-separates body, clothing and accessories into independent pieces (useful for per-part flat materials) — [Tripo P2.0](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/tripo-p2-0-preview); Tripo `generate_parts` does segmentation on H3 — [Tripo docs](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/model-generation/text-to-model-v3-0-v3-1.html)

**Removing baked lighting**
- Meshy `remove_lighting` (Meshy-6/7.1 text-to-3D; Meshy-6 image-to-3D) strips highlights and shadows from base colour — [Meshy API: Image to 3D](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/image-to-3d); [Meshy API: Text to 3D](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/text-to-3d)
- Prevent it at the source with flat neutral concept lighting; use PBR generation; repaint remnants (Tripo Magic Brush) — [Tripo: remove baked lighting](https://www.tripo3d.ai/game-development/remove-baked-lighting-shadows-image-to-3d-ai-game-assets)

**Blender steps (Blender 5.2 LTS manual)**
- Decimate modifier: Collapse (ratio of faces to keep; Symmetry option on one axis; Triangulate), Un-Subdivide (grid topology), Planar ("reduces details on forms comprised of mainly flat surfaces", Angle Limit, Delimit by Normal/Material/Seam/Sharp/UVs) — [Blender Manual: Decimate](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/decimate.html)
- Diffuse bake influence: "If only Color is selected you get the pass color, which is a property of the surface and independent of sampling refinement"; Selected to Active bakes from high-poly to low-poly with Cage/Extrusion/Max Ray Distance; Output Target can be "Image Textures" or "Active Color Attribute" ("Bake to the Active Color Attributes layer on the active mesh") — [Blender 5.2 Manual: Render Baking](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cycles/baking.html)
- Two cleanup paths: quick Decimate (props/background) vs Voxel Remesh + texture bake (clean quads, animation) — [Triverse tutorial, 2026-07-07](https://triverse.ai/blog/clean-up-ai-model-in-blender-tutorial)
- Faceted look: apply Shade Flat in Blender or enable flat shading in the engine import; remesh before UV/texturing/rigging so UVs are not broken — [Meshy tutorial](https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/make-low-poly-3d-models)
- Baking to vertex colours: bake Diffuse without Direct/Indirect, view with Flat lighting and Vertex colour in the viewport — [Victor Karp: bake textures to vertex colors (via search summary)](https://victorkarp.com/how-to-bake-textures-to-vertex-colors-in-blender/)
- Tripo artist checklist after generation: flatten large planar faces, fix n-gons, remove unneeded loops on cylinders, check silhouette; bottom-centre pivot for props — [Tripo blog](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/how-to-generate-low-poly-assets-with-ai)

### Inferences
- Recommended order for the user's toy pipeline: (1) concept image with flat lighting → (2) low-poly mode at a small budget (roughly 1–5K faces for a 2.5-head sprite character, based on the vendors' mobile bands) with texture off or remove-lighting on → (3) Blender: Planar/Collapse decimate if needed, Shade Flat → (4) either assign 4–8 flat pastel materials by selecting face regions (easiest when the generator separated parts), or bake Diffuse Color-only to the Active Color Attribute and quantise it to the palette → (5) add dot eyes/blush as separate small meshes → (6) render sprites with the project's own lights.
- Colour quantisation to a fixed palette is not a built-in Blender bake option in the manual pages I read; it would be a small Python/geometry-nodes step or done on the baked image.

### Gaps
- No primary source found that measures how often each generator ignores "low poly" in the text prompt when low-poly mode is off; evidence is vendor caveats plus the two face counts I inspected.
- No official Blender tool for palette snapping of colour attributes was confirmed.
